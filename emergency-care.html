BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

What to Do When a Dental Problem Happens

These simple steps may help you protect the affected area while arranging professional care. They do not replace a dental examination.

01

Contact a Dental Professional

Explain your symptoms and when they started so the urgency can be assessed.

02

Protect the Affected Area

Avoid chewing on a damaged tooth and do not try to repair or reposition it yourself.

03

Follow Professional Advice

Follow the instructions provided by your dental team while arranging care.

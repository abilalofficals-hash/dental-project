ABOUT DENTAL IMPLANTS

A Reliable Option for Replacing Missing Teeth

Dental implants are small posts placed in the jawbone by a qualified dental professional. They can support replacement teeth and are one option for restoring a missing tooth or several missing teeth.

Your dentist will assess your gums, jawbone, general oral health and treatment goals before deciding whether implants are suitable for you.

PROTECT YOUR SMILE

Looking After Your Dental Implants

Dental implants need regular care, just like natural teeth. Your dental team can provide aftercare instructions suited to your treatment and recovery.

  • Brush and clean between teeth as advised.
  • Attend recommended dental check-ups.
  • Follow your dentist's healing instructions.
  • Report persistent pain, swelling or other concerns.

ABOUT ORTHODONTICS

A Straighter Smile Starts With the Right Care

Orthodontics focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and bite problems. A personalized treatment plan can improve the appearance of your smile and make teeth easier to clean.

At SmileCare Dental Clinic, your dentist will assess your teeth and discuss suitable options based on your needs, oral health and treatment goals.

  • Individual treatment planning
  • Clear guidance at every stage
  • Follow-up care and progress checks

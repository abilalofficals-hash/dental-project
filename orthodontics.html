ABOUT ORTHODONTICS

Helping You Achieve a Balanced, Beautiful Smile

Orthodontic treatment helps correct crowded teeth, gaps, and certain bite problems. Properly aligned teeth can be easier to clean and may improve everyday comfort.

At SmileCare, we assess your dental needs and discuss suitable options, expected treatment time, and how to care for your teeth throughout the process.

  • Individual treatment planning
  • Guidance throughout treatment
  • Advice for maintaining oral hygiene

CARE FOR YOUR SMILE

Keep Your Teeth Healthy During Treatment

Brush carefully twice a day, clean between your teeth, follow appliance-care instructions, and attend your scheduled dental visits. Ask your dental team which cleaning tools are best for your treatment.
